Behind the Brand

Our Story

One brand. One belief — that what you wear is who you are. Streetwear woven with an Egyptian soul.

Born to make everyday clothing feel like identity.

NASIJ (نسيج — “woven fabric”) started with a simple frustration: great streetwear in Egypt was either overpriced imports or forgettable basics. Nothing that felt like us.

So we built a brand around three words: Minimal. Streetwear. Essential. Heavyweight cotton, clean cuts, and graphics that carry a piece of Cairo — the Sphinx, Arabic script, and soul — printed with intention.

Every piece is designed and made in Egypt, for men and women, in sizes S to XXL. Made to be lived in — and to turn heads.

"More than clothing — identity woven in every piece."

Legal & Info

Our Policies

Shipping, returns, privacy, and care — everything you need to know.

Shipping & Delivery

Delivery Areas

We currently deliver across Greater Cairo, Giza, New Cairo, and Sheikh Zayed. If your area isn't listed at checkout, please reach us via WhatsApp.

Delivery Timeframes

  • Ready items — same-day or next-day delivery.
  • Custom / personalised orders — 3 business days production, delivered on day 4.
  • Delivery is by motorcycle courier, door-to-door.

Delivery Fees

Delivery fees are calculated at checkout based on your area. Free delivery on orders above 3,000 EGP.

Order Add-ons

We offer gift wrapping, fresh flowers, and personalised message cards. Select at checkout or mention in the order notes.

Returns & Exchanges

Our Policy

Because every NASIJ piece is handmade to order, we do not accept returns for change of mind. We take great care in quality control before every shipment.

Damaged or Wrong Items

If your order arrives damaged or incorrect, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos via WhatsApp or email. We will arrange a replacement at no cost.

Custom & Personalised Orders

Personalised items (name embroidery, custom colours) are non-refundable unless there is a production defect.

Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

When you place an order or create an account, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and delivery address solely to fulfil your order.

How We Use Your Data

  • Processing and delivering your orders.
  • Sending order confirmations and updates.
  • Improving our products and services.

Data Security

Your data is stored securely and never shared with third parties except our delivery partners. Passwords are encrypted and never stored in plain text.

Contact

For any privacy concerns, email us at hello@naseej.eg or message via WhatsApp.

Care & Materials

Tote Bags & Embroidered Pieces

  • Spot clean with a damp cloth for minor marks.
  • Hand wash cold (max 30°C) with mild detergent if needed.
  • Air dry flat in the shade — avoid direct sunlight.
  • Do not machine wash, tumble dry, or bleach.

Crochet Pieces

  • Hand wash cold with gentle wool-safe detergent.
  • Gently reshape while wet, then lay flat to dry.
  • Do not wring, twist, or hang to dry.

Gloves

  • Hand wash cold, lay flat to dry.
  • Store rolled — not folded — to preserve shape.
  • Keep away from rough surfaces.

Materials Used

All NASIJ pieces use ethically sourced Egyptian cotton, premium wool, and non-toxic dyes. We never use synthetic fillers.

Cookie Policy

Last updated: January 2026

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences and function correctly.

What We Use

  • Necessary cookies — keep your cart, wishlist, and login session active. Without them the site won't work properly.
  • Preference cookies — remember your language choice (Arabic / English) and theme (light / dark).
  • Analytics cookies (if accepted) — help us understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it. No personal data is sold or shared.

What We Don't Do

  • We never sell your data to third parties.
  • We don't use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.
  • We don't share browsing behaviour with anyone outside NASIJ.

Your Choice

When you first visit, a banner lets you choose Accept All (includes preference + analytics cookies) or Necessary Only. You can change your preference at any time by clearing your browser's local storage, or by clicking the button below.

Contact

Questions about our cookie use? Email us at hello@naseej.eg.
